Box Breathing for Calm Focus

Use this simple four-step breathing pattern to calm your nervous system and sharpen focus: Inhale → Hold → Exhale → Hold.

What is box breathing?

Box breathing (four-square breathing) is a controlled breathing method used in high-pressure fields such as the military, sports, and performance. Each side of the “box” is the same length: you inhale, hold, exhale, and hold again for equal counts.

How to start

Begin with 4–4–4–4: inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 4, exhale for 4, and hold for 4. When this feels completely comfortable, you can move to 5 or 6 seconds per side. Never force your breath or chase bigger numbers. Comfort and control come first.

  • Reduces stress and anxiety
  • Improves focus before study, teaching, or exams
  • Works in 2–5 minutes and can be repeated during the day

Safety note

Breathe gently through your nose if possible. Stop immediately if you feel dizzy, tight-chested, or uncomfortable. This is not a competition. The goal is a smooth, steady rhythm that your body can handle easily.
