The Shadow of the Change

Linda was walking home at sunset. The streets were quiet. she passed an old shop. Then she heard a soft groan. A zombie was coming toward her. Its arms were shaking, Its eyes were glowing. Linda ran down the road. More zombies were coming from the houses. They were moving slowly but steadily. Linda was searching for a safe place. She hid behind a broken wall. The zombies walked past her. After some time, the street became silent. Linda stepped out carefully. Suddenly, she saw her own shadow on the wall. It was strange. Her hands were shaking. She realized the truth: she was slowly turning into a zombie too.
