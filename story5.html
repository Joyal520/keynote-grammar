Bloody Mary

Emma was standing in the dark bathroom. Her friends were waiting outside. She whispered “Bloody Mary” three times. The candle was shaking, and the mirror was blury. Emma felt cold air around her. A shape was forming in the glass. A pale face was staring at her. Emma tried to run, but the door was not opening. The face in the mirror was smiling. Suddenly, the mirror cracked, and a hand reached out. Emma screamed. Her friends rushed in and found the room empty. Only one thing remained on the mirror—Emma’s name, written in red. she became Bloody Mary.
