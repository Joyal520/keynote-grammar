Quick Intro

This assessment checks your grammar across 5 sections:

  1. MCQ — choose the correct option
  2. Fill-in-the-Blank — click the correct word from the yellow bank
  3. Matching — click a left item then its right pair
  4. Error identification — Correct or Wrong
  5. Sentence Builder — click yellow words to build the sentence
Each question = 5 points. Total = 100 points. Scoring above 50 triggers celebration.

Controls & Tips
  • Use ← / → and Enter keys for MCQs.
  • Click (tap) words to pick them — they disappear from the bank.
  • Matching locks pairs—continue until all matched.
  • One retry for sentence builder; time limit is strict.
  • Use the Back button to return to this intro at any time.
