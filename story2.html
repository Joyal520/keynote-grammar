The Whisper Behind the Door

Sara moved into an old house on a rainy evening. The hallway was colder than the other rooms, but she ignored it. At midnight, a soft whisper came through the cracks of her bedroom door. She thought it was just the wind. She tried to sleep. But the whisper became louder. She opened the door, the corridor was empty. She noticed a muddy footprint on the floor. The whisper came again, closer this time, right behind her. Sara turned slowly. Her heart started pounding fast. Then she realized she was not alone in the house.
