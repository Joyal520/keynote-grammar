One-Minute Magic Reading

In the heart of an ancient forest lived a tiny bioluminescent mushroom named Lume. Every night, it glowed with an ethereal turquoise light that guided lost fireflies home. One stormy evening, a curious explorer discovered Lume and tried to pluck it. Suddenly, thousands of mushrooms ignited together, creating a breathtaking aurora under the trees. The explorer froze in awe, realizing some wonders must remain untouched. Nature whispered its secret: true beauty belongs to everyone when left in its rightful place.
