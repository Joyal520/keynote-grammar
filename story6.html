Magic Reading

Good bye!

Liam visited the hospital every day. He hoped his father would wake up, even for one moment, to say goodbye. He read small stories and played soft music for his father. One night, Liam held his father’s quiet hand. and whispered, “Please wake up. Please hear me.” The room was silent except for the beeping machines. Then, very slowly, his father’s hand squeezed his hand. Liam looked up. His father opened his eyes for a second. They were full of love and sadness. His lips tried to speak, but no sound came. His hand fell still. Liam cried. In his heart, he heard one word: “Sorry.”
