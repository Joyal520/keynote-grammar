Magic Reading

The watch

Liam found a small silver watch in the garden. It was old, but it was glowing softly. He pressed the top button. Suddenly, he was standing in a busy street from the past. People were wearing strange clothes. Horses were pulling carts. Liam was walking slowly. He was watching everything. He was excited and scared. He pressed the button again. In a flash, he came back to his garden. Liam understood one thing and smiled. He knew the watch could take him anywhere in time. He smiled and held it tightly. His adventure was only beginning.
